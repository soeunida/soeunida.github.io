About

I make large models run faster and smaller on real hardware, from low-bit LLM inference to GPU and NPU deployment for on-vehicle and edge AI.

On the model side I build quantization and distillation methods for Transformers. On the system side I have optimized pipelines with TensorRT on Jetson Orin Nano, pruned and quantized detectors for NPUs, designed in-DRAM and PIM accelerators, and scheduled jobs across GPU-sharing clusters.
